# Illustrations du portfolio

Ces images ont été créées avec l’outil intégré image_gen. Elles illustrent les thèmes du portfolio et ne documentent pas des projets ou des lieux réels. Le modèle Greva sert de référence de composition, sans reprise de ses textes, de son logo ou de ses images.

## 1. trade

Fichier : `assets/images/trade-illustration.webp`

Prompt utilisé :

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: full-width hero background for a serious economics and development portfolio. Create a panoramic editorial photograph of an imagined West African commercial harbour at dawn, container terminal and large cargo cranes predominantly on the right side, ocean and a quiet coastal urban skyline in the distance. Wide composition approximately 2:1. The left third has calm unobstructed water and soft sky suitable for placing website copy over it later. Natural desaturated slate-blue and ink-blue shadows, pale warm sky, copper and rust-coloured containers, restrained daylight. Premium documentary-style architectural photography, realistic scale and fine physical detail, no futuristic elements. No text, logos, labels, charts, watermarks, people in the foreground or identifiable real landmarks. This is an illustrative imagined scene, not evidence of a particular port or project.

## 2. workspace

Fichier : `assets/images/research-desk.webp`

Prompt utilisé :

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: portrait editorial image for the about section of an economist's professional portfolio. A carefully composed natural photograph of a working desk by a sunlit window: an open cream notebook with unlabelled faint pencil marks, a dark ink-blue archival folder, a copper-brown leather folio and a simple graphite pencil on a pale oak surface, the corner of a silver laptop barely entering the right edge. Focus on physical paper, texture and soft shadows. Quiet, believable research workspace, high-end magazine photography, warm paper and muted navy colour palette. Portrait composition approximately 4:5. No legible writing, no invented numerical results or charts, no person, no logos, no graphic overlays, no watermarks.

La vue agricole `assets/images/agriculture-editorial.webp` est une illustration générée lors de la précédente version du portfolio.
